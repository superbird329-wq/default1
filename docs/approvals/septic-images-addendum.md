## 2. The mistake in my packet

Section 5 of the September 3 packet listed "Site elevations" as "Removed from
image." That was not accurate for the site plan. **The elevations are still on
it.**

What is still shown on that image:

- Rim and invert elevations in several of the structure callouts
- A top-of-wall and bottom-of-wall elevation pair
- A spot elevation on the fence line
- Several labelled contour lines crossing the drawing

Section 4 of the same packet was narrower and correct: it said only that the
proposed dwelling's finished floor and basement floor elevations had been
removed. Sections 4 and 5 contradicted each other and I did not catch it.
Section 5 was the one that was wrong, and it is the one written as a
certification.

This matters because elevations and contours are site data in their own right. A
labelled contour set together with the shape of the property line can be enough
to match a parcel in the county GIS viewer even with no name, address, lot
number or title block anywhere on the sheet. So the approval you gave was given
against a description of that image that was not true, and I do not think it
should be relied on. That is my error, not yours.
